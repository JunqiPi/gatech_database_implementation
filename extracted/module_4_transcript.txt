>> In this lesson, we will introduce the idea of

approach of using a single mutex for all the accounts is known
as coarse-grained locking. Separate mutass for each
account allows multiple threads to simultaneously
perform transactions on different accounts
that do not conflict. This approach reduces
the wait times for threads that interact with
different bank accounts, enhancing concurrency, and speeding up overall
transactional throughput. In this C++ snippet, we look at the

account mutass array corresponds to one bank account. During transaction processing,
the respective mutex for account is locked
and unlocked around the operations of
depositing and withdrawing. This approach enhances overall
concurrency by allowing non-conflicting

different bank accounts. It also ensures that the

from other threats. In the performed
